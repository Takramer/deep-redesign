# Real-reader test kit

Five people, one per reader type, 30 minutes each, on their own device if possible. One person runs the session; one takes notes. Do not help. Do not explain the site. Record the screen if they agree.

## Before the sessions

- Readers (one per profile): ___
- Pass marks, written now, not after:
  - Task 1 passes if the reader finds the fitting work in under 60 seconds without help.
  - Task 2 passes if the reader can name one strength and one doubt, both tied to something on the page.
  - Task 3 passes if the reader reaches a working way to get in touch in under 30 seconds.

## Script

**Opening (2 min).** "We are testing the site, not you. Think out loud. There are no wrong answers."

**First look (5 seconds).** Show the home page for five seconds, then hide it.
- What is this site? Who is it for? What would you do next?

**Task 1 (up to 5 min).** "You are <reader's real situation>. Find the work that would help you decide."

**Task 2 (up to 8 min).** "Look at that piece of work. What is good about it? What is unclear or missing?"

**Task 3 (up to 3 min).** "You want to get in touch. Do it." (Stop before they send anything.)

**Wrap-up (5 min).**
- What would you remember about this site tomorrow?
- What made you hesitate?
- What one thing would you change?
- Compared with similar sites you have seen, where does it sit?

## After all five

1. List every problem that stopped a task. Fix those first.
2. List problems that slowed two or more readers.
3. Only then, new ideas.
4. Write what the test cannot tell you (sample size, who recruited them, their devices).
