## 2. Questions for the owner

**Goal**

1. What should a visitor do after reading the site? Rank: hire, buy, contact, read, follow.
2. Who are the two or three kinds of reader that matter most? For each: their job, what they are deciding, how much time they give a site like this.
3. What does the current site get wrong, in your words?
4. What would make this redesign a success six months from now? A number if you have one.

**Taste**

5. Describe the look you want in about a dozen words. Then describe what it must never look like.
6. Three sites you admire and one thing you would take from each. One site you dislike and why.
7. Is there anything you have rejected before that should never come back? (Features, styles, words.)
8. Motion: none, quiet, or a few signature moments? Anything that must never move?

**Content and facts**

9. Which pieces of work, products or pages must be on the site? Which can go?
10. Which facts about you are public, which are private, which are sensitive? (Health, disability, money, clients, employers.)
11. For every number you want shown: where does it come from, and can we check it?
12. Contact: which address, which links, anything to hide from crawlers?

**Constraints**

13. Platform: keep the current one, or open to a move if a test shows it cannot do what the design needs?
14. Budget: hosting plan, paid tools, a ceiling for agent or API spend.
15. Deadline, and anything that must ship first.
16. Who else signs off?

**Working style**

17. How do you want to review: films, stills, live previews? On a phone or a desk?
18. How often do you want a report? (Default: one per round, nothing in between.)
19. Can agents push review branches and open previews without asking? Can they deploy?
20. Anything else an outsider would get wrong about you or this site?
