# Humanizer eval

Run this after the rewrite, yourself, without spawning a separate evaluator. Answer each check pass or fail. Any fail means fix the draft and check again.

For detect requests, only the Detect section applies.

## Never invent

1. Does the edit add zero claims, examples, stats, quotes, names, places, or opinions that were not in the original?
2. Are every number, date, name, and proper noun from the original still correct and unchanged?

If either fails, nothing else matters. Fix it first.

## Editing principles

1. Does the edit preserve the user's point?
2. Does it preserve the writer's distinctive vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, digressions, and level of polish?
3. Does it leave strong human sentences alone instead of rewriting them for consistency?
4. Is the cutting proportional to the actual slop, with no compression that strips out character?
5. Does the draft lead with what the reader needs while keeping personal setup that adds context, tension, or character?
6. Do sentences earn their place, with concrete facts, protected details, and direct verbs?
7. Does every generic sentence pass the portability test, or was it cut or made specific?
8. Does the draft use active voice with human subjects where possible?
9. Are genuinely tangled sentences fixed while clear spoken cadence, fragments, and changes in pace remain intact?

## Words and patterns

1. Are banned words, filler phrases, empty adverbs, and inflated claims gone, unless quoted as examples?
2. Are binary parallelisms, negative listings, rhetorical setups, and throat-clearing openers removed?
3. Are faux-insight setups, colon reveals, superficial -ing analyses, copula avoidance, synonym cycling, and staccato drama fixed?
4. Are importance puffery and weasel attribution replaced with plain facts and named sources, or flagged when no source exists?
5. Is interpretive metadiscourse removed?
6. Are fake-profound kickers deleted rather than rewritten into better metaphors?
7. Are generic positive conclusions and summary-recap endings cut, so the piece ends on a concrete point, takeaway, or next action?
8. Is formatting slop gone: emoji headings, decorative bold, bullets that should be prose, headers over two-sentence sections, title case headings?
9. Does the final text contain zero em dashes and en dashes? Scan for the literal characters. Any hit is a fail.
10. Are quotation marks straight, not curly?

## Final read

1. Does the draft avoid robotic symmetry, repeated sentence shapes, and stacked punchy fragments?
2. Would the writer recognize the edited draft as their own voice?
3. Would it sound natural read aloud to a sharp colleague?
4. Did you check the false-positive list before flattening anything that might just be a human writing well?
5. Does the output include the final rewrite and a short **What changed** section?

## Detect

1. Does the response name each pattern found, quote the line, and give a short fix?
2. Does it avoid rewriting the draft, scoring it, or claiming AI authorship?
